About the course

A seminar on NeuroAI, the joint study of artificial and biological intelligence, for advanced undergraduates and graduate students. The course covers how modern deep networks and foundation models are trained, what interpretability methods reveal about them, and how well they match brains and behavior.

Each topic has required reading for everyone. Students then present recent papers on the topic in short talks.
